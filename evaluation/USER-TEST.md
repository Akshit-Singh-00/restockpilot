# Fifteen-minute café operator test

Status: protocol prepared; no participants recruited or results claimed.

Ask the participant for permission to take notes. Recording, quotes, names and publication each need their agreement. Use synthetic inventory; do not collect customer records or payment credentials.

## Opening

"We are testing this prototype, not you. Please think aloud. You can stop at any time. This uses sample products and PayPal sandbox funds only."

Ask how they currently order supplies, their most recent stockout, and their usual ordering constraints. Avoid pitching expected benefits first.

## Tasks

1. Using the same inventory sheet, choose a restock cart with a $200 budget using their normal method. Time the task.
2. In RestockPilot, find which product will run out before delivery and explain the chart in their own words.
3. Generate a plan, explain one recommendation, change a quantity and identify the exact total before checkout. Time this task separately.
4. Ask what they expect to happen if they exceed the budget or change the cart after approving it. Have them try.
5. Review the recorded sandbox payment and explain the difference between buyer approval and confirmed capture. Do not ask them for real payment details.

Alternate which ordering method participants try first. Use equivalent scenarios to reduce practice effects. Do not treat a five-person usability test as proof of commercial savings.
